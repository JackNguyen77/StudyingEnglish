## 1. Story

A short report described the local impact in clear and simple language.

The main reason was the rising cost, but the details were more complex than the headline.

People were worried about the small businesses because it could change daily life.

The report also mentioned the support program as an important detail.

Experts said the community need would matter over the next few months.

The story helped me see who is affected instead of only reading the headline.
