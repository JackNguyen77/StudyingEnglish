# Story 133 - Chuyển ghi âm thành chữ

Audio: [133-voice-note-transcript.mp3](../audio/133-voice-note-transcript.mp3)

## 1. Story

I recorded a voice note because typing on the bus was hard.

The speech-to-text feature turned my words into a rough draft.

Background noise made two sentences look strange on the screen.

I edited the text until I had a clean transcript.

I had to correct a word that sounded close but meant something else.

Then I listened back to make sure the idea was still mine.

That small habit made the tool feel useful without taking over my thinking.
