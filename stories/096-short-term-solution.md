## 1. Story

A short report described the short-term solution in clear and simple language.

The main reason was the immediate problem, but the details were more complex than the headline.

People were worried about the limited budget because it could change daily life.

The report also mentioned the temporary measure as an important detail.

Experts said the follow-up plan would matter over the next few months.

The story helped me reduce the pressure instead of only reading the headline.
