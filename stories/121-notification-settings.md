# Story 121 - Điều chỉnh thông báo

Audio: [121-notification-settings.mp3](../audio/121-notification-settings.mp3)

## 1. Story

I opened the notification settings because my phone kept interrupting my study time.

I turned off alerts from apps that were not important during work.

Too many pop-ups made it hard to focus on one task.

I used focus mode so only urgent messages could come through.

I kept important updates on, but I moved everything else to later.

By the end, I could check later without feeling pulled all day.

That small change made my phone feel calmer the next time I picked it up.
