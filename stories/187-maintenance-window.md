# Story 187 - Chọn maintenance window

## 1. Story

I checked the maintenance window before I made a final choice.

One business hour conflict made the situation less simple than it looked.

I decided to schedule the downtime before I moved forward.

Then I wrote a short downtime note so the other person understood me.

I also needed to confirm the fallback plan instead of guessing.

That helped me reduce disruption without adding extra stress.

By the end, the whole situation felt calmer and easier to handle.
