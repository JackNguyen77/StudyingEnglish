## 1. Story

A short report described the education plan in clear and simple language.

The main reason was the learning gap, but the details were more complex than the headline.

People were worried about the teacher training because it could change daily life.

The report also mentioned the school support as an important detail.

Experts said the student progress would matter over the next few months.

The story helped me see the access problem instead of only reading the headline.
