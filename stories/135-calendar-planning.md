# Story 135 - Sắp lịch thông minh

Audio: [135-calendar-planning.mp3](../audio/135-calendar-planning.mp3)

## 1. Story

I sent a calendar invite after we agreed on the time.

The time zone was different, so I checked it twice.

The app suggested an available slot that worked for everyone.

I kept a meeting reminder ten minutes before the call.

I moved one task to avoid overlap with another meeting.

When the plan changed, I sent an update instead of a new invite.

That small habit made the tool feel useful without taking over my thinking.
