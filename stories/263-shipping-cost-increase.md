## 1. Story

I listened to the shipping cost increase before I shared my view.

One fuel surcharge made the update harder to explain.

I tried to compare delivery routes instead of reacting too quickly.

Then I wrote a short shipping cost note so the facts stayed clear.

I also had to check supplier contracts before I repeated the claim.

That helped me control operating costs without making the conversation tense.

By the end, I could explain the issue in plain English.
