## 1. Story

A short report described the long-term effect in clear and simple language.

The main reason was the early warning, but the details were more complex than the headline.

People were worried about the health risk because it could change daily life.

The report also mentioned the prevention plan as an important detail.

Experts said the future cost would matter over the next few months.

The story helped me think beyond today instead of only reading the headline.
