## 1. Story

I listened to the local tax proposal before I shared my view.

One resident pushback made the update harder to explain.

I tried to review public comments instead of reacting too quickly.

Then I wrote a short tax proposal note so the facts stayed clear.

I also had to check revenue needs before I repeated the claim.

That helped me fund essential services without making the conversation tense.

By the end, I could explain the issue in plain English.

Tôi nghe proposal thuế địa phương trước khi chia sẻ góc nhìn của mình.

Một pushback từ cư dân làm phần update khó giải thích hơn.

Sau đó tôi viết một ghi chú proposal thuế ngắn để các facts rõ ràng.
