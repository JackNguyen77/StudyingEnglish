# Story 181 - Hỏi trong discovery call

## 1. Story

I checked the discovery question before I made a final choice.

One vague answer made the situation less simple than it looked.

I decided to ask a follow up before I moved forward.

Then I wrote a short call summary so the other person understood me.

I also needed to confirm the business need instead of guessing.

That helped me understand the real problem without adding extra stress.

By the end, the whole situation felt calmer and easier to handle.
