## 1. Story

A short report described the budget plan in clear and simple language.

The main reason was the funding gap, but the details were more complex than the headline.

People were worried about the spending limit because it could change daily life.

The report also mentioned the priority area as an important detail.

Experts said the cost review would matter over the next few months.

The story helped me understand budget choices instead of only reading the headline.
