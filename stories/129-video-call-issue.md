# Story 129 - Sửa lỗi video call

Audio: [129-video-call-issue.mp3](../audio/129-video-call-issue.mp3)

## 1. Story

The video call link opened, but the room stayed blank.

A poor connection made everyone sound broken and far away.

I muted the mic while I checked the router behind me.

My teammate shared the link again in case I opened the wrong one.

I switched to mobile data and the picture became clear.

After that, I rejoined the call and explained the delay.

That small change made my phone feel calmer the next time I picked it up.
