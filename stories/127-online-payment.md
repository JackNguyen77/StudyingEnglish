# Story 127 - Thanh toán online

Audio: [127-online-payment.mp3](../audio/127-online-payment.mp3)

## 1. Story

I chose a payment method before I confirmed the order.

The first payment failed, but the app did not explain why.

I had to update card details because the old card had expired.

A digital receipt arrived after the second try went through.

I checked the transaction history to make sure I paid only once.

When the page froze again, I decided to try again later.

That small change made my phone feel calmer the next time I picked it up.
