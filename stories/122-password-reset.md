# Story 122 - Đặt lại mật khẩu

Audio: [122-password-reset.mp3](../audio/122-password-reset.mp3)

## 1. Story

I started a password reset after I forgot the old one.

The app sent a verification code to my email right away.

For a minute, I felt locked out and worried about my account.

I followed the steps to recover access without asking support.

I chose a new password that I could remember but not guess easily.

After that, I could sign back in and finish the payment.

That extra check made the account feel safer the next time I opened the app.

## 2. Dịch tự nhiên

Tôi bắt đầu đặt lại mật khẩu sau khi quên mật khẩu cũ.
