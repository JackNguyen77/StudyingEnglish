## 1. Story

I pasted a long article into the tool because I felt lost.

The summary showed the key points in a few short lines.

I saved the summary note so I could review it later.

One missing detail changed the meaning of the whole paragraph.

I still needed to double-check facts before I trusted the result.

After fixing it, I saved the summary in my study folder.

That small habit made the tool feel useful without taking over my thinking.

## 3. Cụm cần học

### 1. long article: bài viết dài

Tôi dán một bài viết dài vào công cụ vì thấy bị lạc.

### Cụm phụ trong bài

- felt lost -> felt lost (thấy bị lạc) - I felt lost in the article.
- a few short lines -> ờ phiu soọt lainz (vài dòng ngắn) - It used a few short lines.
- whole paragraph -> hâu paragraph (cả đoạn) - It changed the whole paragraph.
- study folder -> sta-đi folder (thư mục học) - I saved it in my study folder.
