# Story 147 - Đi theo bản đồ

Audio: [147-map-navigation.mp3](../audio/147-map-navigation.mp3)

## 1. Story

The map showed a route option that avoided the main road.

A traffic delay added fifteen minutes to the trip.

I sent my estimated arrival to my friend before I left.

Turn-by-turn directions helped me stay calm in a new area.

The app had to reroute when one street was closed.

I saved the place so I could find it again next time.

That quick check made the trip feel smoother and less stressful.
