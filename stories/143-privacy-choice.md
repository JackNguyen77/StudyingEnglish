# Story 143 - Chọn quyền riêng tư

Audio: [143-privacy-choice.mp3](../audio/143-privacy-choice.mp3)

## 1. Story

The app showed a privacy choice before I created an account.

I did not want to share personal information that the app did not need.

The data collection note explained what the company would save.

I chose to opt out of tracking for ads.

The privacy policy was long, so I looked for the plain summary.

Later, I opened settings to manage consent again.

That backup habit made my files easier to trust the next time I needed them.
