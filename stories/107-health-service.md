## 1. Story

A short report described the health service in clear and simple language.

The main reason was the patient demand, but the details were more complex than the headline.

People were worried about the staff shortage because it could change daily life.

The report also mentioned the service improvement as an important detail.

Experts said the appointment backlog would matter over the next few months.

The story helped me understand waiting time instead of only reading the headline.
