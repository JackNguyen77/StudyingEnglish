# Story 277 - Chuẩn bị crisis communication

Audio: [277-crisis-communication-plan.mp3](../audio/277-crisis-communication-plan.mp3)

## 1. Story

I walked through the crisis communication playbook before the meeting started.

One public confusion made the decision harder than expected.

I tried to draft holding statements so the team had a clearer path.

Then I sent a short crisis communication note to capture the key points.

I also had to check spokesperson availability before we agreed on the next step.

That helped us control the message without losing momentum.

By the end, the discussion felt sharper and more honest.
