## 1. Story

A short report described the recent change in clear and simple language.

The main reason was the policy shift, but the details were more complex than the headline.

People were worried about the daily routine because it could change daily life.

The report also mentioned the official guidance as an important detail.

Experts said the adjustment period would matter over the next few months.

The story helped me follow the change instead of only reading the headline.
