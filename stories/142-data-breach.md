# Story 142 - Xử lý cảnh báo bảo mật

## 1. Story

I read about a data breach and checked whether my email was involved.

A suspicious email asked me to click a link and update my password.

I changed passwords for the accounts that used the same old one.

Then I checked account activity and looked for anything strange.

The security warning was scary, but it gave me clear steps.

I reported the issue so the service could block the fake page.

That backup habit made my files easier to trust the next time I needed them.
