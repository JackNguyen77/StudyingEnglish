## 1. Story

A short report described the supply issue in clear and simple language.

The main reason was the delayed shipment, but the details were more complex than the headline.

People were worried about the low supply because it could change daily life.

The report also mentioned the backup source as an important detail.

Experts said the delivery schedule would matter over the next few months.

The story helped me understand the shortage risk instead of only reading the headline.
