## 1. Story

I listened to the food safety recall before I shared my view.

One contaminated batch made the update harder to explain.

I tried to remove affected products instead of reacting too quickly.

Then I wrote a short recall notice note so the facts stayed clear.

I also had to check store instructions before I repeated the claim.

That helped me keep customers safe without making the conversation tense.

By the end, I could explain the issue in plain English.
