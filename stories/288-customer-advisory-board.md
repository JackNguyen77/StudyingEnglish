## 1. Story

I walked through the customer advisory board before the meeting started.

One mixed feedback made the decision harder than expected.

I tried to group themes clearly so the team had a clearer path.

Then I sent a short advisory board note to capture the key points.

I also had to check product requests before we agreed on the next step.

That helped us turn feedback into priorities without losing momentum.

By the end, the discussion felt sharper and more honest.
