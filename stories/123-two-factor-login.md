# Story 123 - Đăng nhập hai lớp

Audio: [123-two-factor-login.mp3](../audio/123-two-factor-login.mp3)

## 1. Story

The bank app asked for two-factor login before it opened my account.

I typed the security code slowly because one wrong number could block me.

My phone was a trusted device, so the process was faster.

A warning showed a login attempt from a city I did not know.

I did not approve the request until I checked the location.

That extra step helped me protect the account without much effort.

That extra check made the account feel safer the next time I opened the app.
