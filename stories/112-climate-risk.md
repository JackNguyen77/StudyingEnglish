## 1. Story

A short report described the climate risk in clear and simple language.

The main reason was the extreme weather, but the details were more complex than the headline.

People were worried about the flood damage because it could change daily life.

The report also mentioned the early warning system as an important detail.

Experts said the emergency response would matter over the next few months.

The story helped me prepare for change instead of only reading the headline.
