## 1. Story

I listened to the school attendance problem before I shared my view.

One family pressure made the update harder to explain.

I tried to contact support staff instead of reacting too quickly.

Then I wrote a short attendance note so the facts stayed clear.

I also had to check early warning signs before I repeated the claim.

That helped me keep students engaged without making the conversation tense.

By the end, I could explain the issue in plain English.

### Cụm phụ trong bài

- shared my view -> she(r)d mai viu (chia sẻ góc nhìn của tôi) - I shared my view.

- repeated the claim -> ri-pi-tịt đờ klâym (lặp lại claim đó) - I repeated the claim after checking.
- plain English -> plâyn ing-glish (tiếng Anh đơn giản, dễ hiểu) - I explained it in plain English.
