# Story 271 - Chuẩn bị brief cho board decision

Audio: [271-board-decision-brief.mp3](../audio/271-board-decision-brief.mp3)

## 1. Story

I walked through the board decision brief before the meeting started.

One unclear recommendation made the decision harder than expected.

I tried to frame the options so the team had a clearer path.

Then I sent a short decision brief note to capture the key points.

I also had to check financial exposure before we agreed on the next step.

That helped us support a confident decision without losing momentum.

By the end, the discussion felt sharper and more honest.
