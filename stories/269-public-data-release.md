## 1. Story

I listened to the public dataset release before I shared my view.

One missing context made the update harder to explain.

I tried to clean the dataset instead of reacting too quickly.

Then I wrote a short data release note so the facts stayed clear.

I also had to check privacy safeguards before I repeated the claim.

That helped me make findings trustworthy without making the conversation tense.

By the end, I could explain the issue in plain English.
