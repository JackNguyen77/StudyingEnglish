# Story 148 - Luyện trong app học tiếng Anh

Audio: [148-language-app-practice.mp3](../audio/148-language-app-practice.mp3)

## 1. Story

My daily streak reminded me to open the language app.

I set a practice goal that felt small enough to finish.

Pronunciation feedback showed which sentence I needed to repeat.

I repeated the sentence until the rhythm felt smoother.

At night, I reviewed mistakes instead of starting a new lesson.

The small win helped me keep learning even on a busy day.

That small practice loop made the lesson easier to remember the next day.

## 2. Dịch tự nhiên

Tôi đặt mục tiêu luyện tập đủ nhỏ để hoàn thành.
