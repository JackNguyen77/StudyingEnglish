# Story 134 - Tạo nhắc nhở thông minh

Audio: [134-smart-reminder.mp3](../audio/134-smart-reminder.mp3)

## 1. Story

I created a smart reminder for a task I kept forgetting.

It became a recurring task that appeared every Monday morning.

I added a due date so the reminder felt more real.

A push notification appeared before I opened social media.

After I finished, I marked it done and moved on.

If I was busy, I could snooze it for thirty minutes.

That small habit made the tool feel useful without taking over my thinking.
