## 1. Story

I walked through the regional sales pipeline before the meeting started.

One stalled opportunity made the decision harder than expected.

I tried to challenge forecast assumptions so the team had a clearer path.

Then I sent a short pipeline review note to capture the key points.

I also had to check deal health before we agreed on the next step.

That helped us focus sales effort without losing momentum.

By the end, the discussion felt sharper and more honest.
