## 1. Story

I walked through the product quality escalation before the meeting started.

One repeat defect made the decision harder than expected.

I tried to trace failure patterns so the team had a clearer path.

Then I sent a short quality escalation note to capture the key points.

I also had to check replacement options before we agreed on the next step.

That helped us protect customer confidence without losing momentum.

By the end, the discussion felt sharper and more honest.
