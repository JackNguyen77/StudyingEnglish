## 1. Story

A short report described the future plan in clear and simple language.

The main reason was the long-term goal, but the details were more complex than the headline.

People were worried about the key challenge because it could change daily life.

The report also mentioned the action roadmap as an important detail.

The story helped me prepare for what comes next instead of only reading the headline.
