## 1. Story

A short report described the environmental impact in clear and simple language.

The main reason was the waste problem, but the details were more complex than the headline.

People were worried about the clean-up effort because it could change daily life.

Experts said the long-term damage would matter over the next few months.

The story helped me see why protection matters instead of only reading the headline.
